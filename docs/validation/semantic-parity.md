# Semantic Parity

## Purpose

Structural and row-level equivalence cannot prove that advanced workloads preserve business meaning.

## Examples

Semantic validation may include:

- identical population inclusion/exclusion
- financial reconciliation
- measure calculation equivalence
- risk score tolerance
- statistical coefficient comparison
- confidence interval comparison
- model-output comparison
- domain-specific acceptance tests

## Ownership

Semantic acceptance criteria should be established with workload/domain owners for high-risk workloads. The factory can automate execution of these tests, but it should not invent business acceptance criteria.
