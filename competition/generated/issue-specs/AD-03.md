# AD-03 - Macro discovery and expansion strategy

## Business purpose
Humana's paid competition is won on successfully converted AND validated SAS workloads against real Humana data. Generated code alone is not success.

## Competition priority
**P0**. Disposition: **MODIFY**.

## Desired behavior
Implement Macro discovery and expansion strategy to advance the competition critical loop and emit reconstructable evidence.

AD-03 must make macro-dependent behavior explicit enough for workload grouping, readiness,
conversion routing, and Humana pilot triage. It must not claim that static discovery is equivalent to
executing the SAS macro processor.

## Scope boundary

Before implementation begins, the Issue must choose and record whether this increment delivers a
bounded executable expander, a versioned expansion-strategy contract, or both. The accepted scope
must identify:

- supported macro definitions, literal invocations, variables, quoting forms, and `%include`
  interactions;
- treatment of SAS intrinsic/system macro operations;
- treatment of unique, missing, duplicate, ambiguous, dynamic, recursive, and cyclic references;
- deterministic recursion, output-size, and resource limits for any executable expansion;
- the exact unresolved states that block readiness or downstream conversion;
- explicit unsupported constructs, including runtime-dependent macro behavior that cannot be
  resolved from governed evidence.

This increment delivers a versioned expansion-strategy contract with deterministic macro discovery and resolution evidence. Executable SAS macro expansion is out of scope; unresolved or runtime-dependent behavior must remain explicit and fail closed.

### Application to AD-03

AD-03 must:

- distinguish SAS macro-processor constructs, `PROC FORMAT` definitions, and target-language templating constructs as separate artifact and relationship types;
- preserve source-faithful behavior without silently correcting legacy semantics;
- bind every resolved relationship to exact source bytes, policy/strategy version, and attributable evidence;
- invalidate or supersede derived resolution state when its source checksum or strategy version changes;
- expose ambiguity, contradiction, unsupported behavior, and unresolved runtime dependencies rather than selecting a plausible answer;
- treat recalled or externally derived knowledge as non-authoritative context that cannot independently authorize readiness or conversion;
- introduce no runtime network dependency, external embedding call, transcript capture, automatic hook installation, or background scheduler.

## Architecture
Preserve bounded task-specific LLM context separately from durable estate-wide intelligence. Reuse canonical identity, dependency/lineage evidence, provenance, secret sanitization, protected paths, isolated worktrees and Architect/Developer/Reviewer gates. No Humana-only fork.

## Inputs and outputs
Consume canonical workload/run identity, required source/dependency evidence, relevant durable estate intelligence, configured runtime bindings and prior-attempt evidence. Emit deterministic machine-readable state/evidence for orchestration, validation, benchmarking, audit reconstruction, competition telemetry and 2027 scale.

Macro evidence must include stable artifact identity, source checksum, source location, normalized
macro identity, observation/resolution kind, candidate definition identities, expansion strategy and
version when applicable, limits applied, and attributable unresolved/blocker reasons. A resolved
edge or expansion must be reconstructable from persisted inputs and policy.

## Failure, retry and concurrency
Fail closed on missing prerequisites. Separate Humana/environment blockers from conversion, execution and validation defects. Never count blocked, skipped, generated-only or unvalidated work as parity success. Independent workloads must not require global serialization. Retries are bounded and idempotent where applicable.

## Security and evidence
Preserve secret/PHI/PII controls. Credentials are references, never plaintext evidence. Capture workload/run/attempt IDs, source hashes, strategy/provider/model versions when applicable, generated hashes, execution result, validation policy/result, repairs, elapsed/machine time, human intervention, complexity, blockers and terminal disposition.

## Non-goals
No hard-coded demo outcomes, weakened validation, hidden differences, fabricated parity, skipped-as-success behavior or parallel Humana product architecture.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Positive, negative and adversarial fixtures prove behavior.
2. Every terminal outcome is machine-readable and reconstructable.
3. One workload failure cannot corrupt another.
4. Missing prerequisites become explicit blocked/unresolved outcomes.
5. Security regression tests remain green and secrets never persist in canonical evidence.
6. Public corpus source bytes remain untouched.
7. `make verify` and affected confidence gates pass.
8. Telemetry distinguishes generated, executed, validated, parity-passed, human-assisted and blocked outcomes.
9. Idempotency/restart behavior is tested where lifecycle state changes.
10. Bounded-context/durable-intelligence separation is preserved.
11. Unique literal macro definitions and calls resolve deterministically with source-located evidence.
12. Missing, duplicate, ambiguous, dynamic, recursive, cyclic, and limit-exceeding cases remain
    explicit and cannot silently authorize readiness or conversion.
13. SAS intrinsic/system operations are not fabricated as user-macro dependencies unless an
    inventoried user definition establishes that relationship.
14. Macro quoting and SAS-looking text in comments or protected literals do not fabricate calls,
    variables, includes, or expanded source.
15. Any executable expansion is bounded, deterministic, versioned, idempotent, and bound to exact
    source bytes; unsupported runtime-dependent behavior fails closed.

## Required tests
Unit, integration, negative/adversarial, evidence/provenance, security, restart/idempotency and
concurrency tests as applicable, including mutations for ambiguous duplicate definitions, dynamic
macro calls, recursion/cycles, quoting/comment false positives, macro-generated includes and dataset
names, source-byte changes, expansion limits, and false resolved/expanded claims.

## Definition of done
Implementation, tests, docs and evidence gates complete; no gate weakened; Reviewer accepts; final integration remains human-controlled.
